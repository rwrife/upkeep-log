# Store listing and privacy disclosure

## Listing copy

**Name:** Upkeep Log

**Subtitle / short description:** Local household maintenance schedules and
portable service history—without an account.

**Full description:**

Upkeep Log keeps routine household maintenance on your device. Schedule one-time
or recurring tasks, see what is due or upcoming, record notes, parts, costs, and
optional photos, and browse a durable history by asset. Export selected history
as CSV or create a versioned ZIP backup you control.

Core use requires no account, subscription, advertising, analytics, or network
connection. Optional reminders are scheduled locally and may be delayed or
suppressed by the operating system. They are convenience aids, not emergency,
code-compliance, predictive-failure, or equipment-safety monitoring.

**Category:** Productivity (primary); Lifestyle (secondary where supported)

**Keywords:** home maintenance, upkeep, service history, household, reminders,
asset log, offline

## Store privacy answers

- Data is stored in the app-private database and attachment directory.
- Upkeep Log does not transmit user data to the developer or third parties.
- No data is used for tracking, advertising, analytics, profiling, or account
  identity.
- User-entered addresses, notes, serial numbers, costs, and attachments may be
  present locally and in user-directed exports.
- Camera access starts only from **Attach → Take photo**.
- Photo selection starts only from **Attach → Choose photo**.
- File import/export and the share sheet start only from explicit user actions.
- Notification authorization is requested only after the user enables a local
  reminder.
- No location, contacts, microphone, Bluetooth, broad storage, push, or Internet
  permission is used.
- Deleting the app removes app-private data subject to OS backup/restore policy;
  in-app per-home deletion and full reset require confirmation.

The owner must compare these answers with the final store forms and generated
platform privacy reports before submission. Store policy wording can change;
this file is product truth, not proof that a submission was completed.
